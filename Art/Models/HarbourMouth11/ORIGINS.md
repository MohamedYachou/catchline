# Source origins and preservation

ART-011 geometry, composition and builder are authored specifically for Mo's Catchline assignment. Catchline's existing owned Geometry, mesh/material and FBX helpers are reused. The existing Rowboat, props and Captain rig are referenced in the review scene only and remain unchanged.

Read-only Plunder inspection authorized by Mo:

- `tools/blender/islands/scenery_cays.py` and the Gull Rock source: connected terrace/backbone composition and attached fracture slabs.
- `tools/blender/ptsb/island_kit.py`: broad offset rock profiles and restrained erosion methodology.
- `tools/blender/ptsb/cay_biome.py`: continuous terraces/submerged land rather than isolated generic boulders.
- `tools/blender/out/islands/gull_rock.blend`, `smugglers_hook.blend` and their existing rendered reviews: inspected source presence and actual visual results.
- Plunder `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `INDEX.md`, status, island skill and island-style guidance were read before inspection.

The shared design lessons inform this new temperate, metre-scale composition. No Plunder builder, Blender object, texture, material library or third-party kit file is copied into the export, and Plunder is not modified. This delivery introduces no third-party asset license obligation. The referenced Caribbean foliage/settlement design is not transplanted into Gull Harbour. Inspected source-file hashes are retained in `plunder-readonly-hashes.json` and rechecked by validation. External free/commercial asset downloads were not needed.

`Runtime-HUD-source-*.png` are retained copies of Catchline's native POLISH-002 bite captures solely for labelled footprint composites. They are not new UI assets and do not establish acceptance of the rejected runtime presentation.
