HARBOUR-ART-1 — Codex 2 (WSL artist) — the growing Gull Harbour kit and the sea-lane landmarks

GOAL (Mo): "tier 5 is too big for the harbour", "the base upgrades with tiers", old boats stay moored, departure
past the lighthouse and breakwater. Design: docs/plans/HARBOUR-PLAN.md (read it first). Claude codes the layout
and places every model by name; a model that is missing is skipped, so deliver in the ORDER below, one at a time.

CONTEXT: docs/plans/HARBOUR-PLAN.md, Art/STYLE.md, the existing kit Art/Models/GullHarbour/Harbour/*.blend
(Quay4m, Lighthouse, BreakwaterRock*, HouseA/B, ShopFront_*, MarketStallAwning, Seagull) and its FBX export
conventions (Unity axes, metres, origin at ground contact, the front faces -Z = toward the water, +Z = land).

HARBOUR FRAME (where Claude places things; build to these sizes): x runs along the shore, water is z < 2.3, the quay
top is y 0.35, the waterline is y -0.32. Boats: Rowboat 3x2 m, Skiff 5x3, Compact 4.3x2.3, Prep 5.7x2.9, Buoy fisher
7.3x3.5.

ORDER, exact model names, one .blend + .fbx each in Art/Models/GullHarbour/HarbourV2/:
A. Structures
   - Slipway: concrete-and-sand ramp, 8 m wide, 7 m long, from y 0.35 down to -0.9, with rails for two rowboats.
   - PontoonSegment: floating, 2 m wide x 4 m, top y 0.0, with cleats, rubber fender and floats showing.
   - PontoonGangway: hinged ramp 3 m long, from the quay top down to the pontoon.
   - FuelDock: a 2x2 m float with a fuel pump, hose and a small sign.
   - QuayCrane: about 5 m tall, the jib a child object named "Jib" (Claude swings it), hook and rope.
   - BreakwaterWall: a 4 m armour-rock segment with a 1.8 m concrete cap path, top y 0.6.
   - BreakwaterEnd: a rounded head.
   - OuterWharf: a 4 m concrete wharf segment for the breakwater's inner face, 1.8 m deep, two bollards.
   - WharfLight.
   - MouthBeacon: the green harbour-mouth light on a rock base. The existing Lighthouse is the red side.
B. Buildings that unlock with the tiers
   - HarbourMasterHut: a 2.5x2 m timber hut with a window and a flag.
   - NoticeBoard: 2 m wide, roofed, papers pinned. It hosts the crew tab before the crew office exists.
   - SuppliesShed: 3x2.5 m, open front, with sacks, fuel cans and bait boxes.
   - IceHouse: about 3.5x3 m, stone, with a thick insulated door and an ice chute.
   - NetLoft: tall and timber, nets hanging from the upper door.
   - BoatyardShed: an open-sided timber shed, about 9x5 m, for a boat on a cradle.
   - BoatCradle: timber blocks and props that hold a hull up to 7.5 m.
C. Other fishermen's boats: colour variants of the existing hulls, same mesh, the hull and cabin paint swapped.
   Names: CompactFisherV1_VariantRed, PrepFisherV1_VariantGreen, Tier5DeepLines103_VariantBlue,
   Skiff_VariantYellow. Take the sources from wherever the current boat FBXs were built.
D. Sea-lane landmarks and moments
   - ChannelMarkerRed and ChannelMarkerGreen: spar buoys with topmarks.
   - BellBuoy.
   - Wreck: a half-sunk 8 m fishing hull.
   - SealRocks: a rock group with three seals lying on it, 6 m across.
   - FloatingCrate.
   - Dolphin: one mesh, 2.2 m, a smooth leaping pose. Claude animates the arc.
   - SeagullDive: wings folded, diving.
   - HandCart: a dock hand-truck with two fish crates.

building and 1.5k per prop, no loose material folders. Match the scale of the existing kit, using Quay4m and
HouseA as rulers.
