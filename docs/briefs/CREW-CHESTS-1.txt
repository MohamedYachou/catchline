1. Card face (one reusable uGUI component, used in the crew office, deploy list, chest reveal): the frame for the
   card's rarity, the portrait in the window, name in the band (cream, Skin heading), role + level small, the three
   ability rows (blue/purple/gold ability text; rows above the current rarity dimmed), a copies bar
   "7 / 10 copies" under the frame. Locked frame for cards not yet unlocked (silhouette portrait).
2. Copies replace role wildcards as the upgrade cost: Blue->Purple 10 copies + the existing coins, Purple->Gold 25

4. A Gold card's extra copies each become 1 blue wildcard immediately (toast "Mira is maxed: +2 wildcards").
5. Exchange in the crew office: 10 blue -> 1 purple, 10 purple -> 1 gold (buttons, enabled at 10).
6. Chests (Crew office tab, a "Chests" row): one chest per boat tier up to the current hull tier.

   Copies are drawn only from cards the player owns (unlocked by tier), uniform per copy, seeded from State.Rng.
   Tier-N chests add a 10% chance per copy of 1 bonus blue wildcard instead (never more than 1 per chest).
7. Opening (mobile, tap only): the chest sits centred and shakes; tap opens it; cards fly out and stack face down;
   each tap flips the next card (scale-pop, rarity glow), then a summary grid "2x Mira, 1x Noor, +1 wildcard" with
   Done. Skip button reveals all. Sounds: existing Purchase/Coins/Click cues.
