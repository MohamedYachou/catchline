FISH-SILHOUETTE-1 — Codex 1 (Windows) — underwater fish silhouettes from the real 3D models (groundwork for the new rod minigame)

GOAL: the new fishing minigame (Claude designs it) shows the fish under the water before it bites: Mo wants
"really showing a fish ... going for the rod and faking and doing a nibble", "bigger fish should also be bigger",
"the outline of the 3d model we're catching would be nice". Build the reusable rendering piece only, no gameplay.

BUILD: a runtime component FishShadow (Assets/Scripts/Game/Minigame/FishShadow.cs) that, given a Species and kg,
spawns that species' existing 3D model (same lookup the catch card / basket fish use: ArtLibrary), scales it by
real size (length from kg; reuse GameData min/max kg), and renders it as an underwater silhouette: dark tinted body
that fades with depth (0 = touching the surface, 1 = deep and faint) plus a soft light outline/rim that stays
readable over the PTS water. Expose Depth (0..1), Visibility, and simple swim API: SwimTo(worldPos, speed) with a
tail-wag/body-bend (shader vertex wave or bone wiggle, whichever the model supports) and Dart(dir) for a sudden
burst. URP; a Shader Graph or HLSL shader in Assets/Scripts/Game/Minigame/. No new art assets.

OWNERSHIP: Assets/Scripts/Game/Minigame/** (new), an Editor preview/check in Assets/Editor/FishShadowChecks.cs.
Do not touch VoyageDeck/VoyageInterface/Sea/** or saves. Scratch Unity copy Catchline-lead-sim only; never the main
project. No git commits.
