DEFECTS (from Claude's code review; fix in this order, finish and log one before starting the next):
1. Guests slide. During Seating they stand still at the door until Mira's whole task ends. Then they MoveTowards the
   seat in a straight line through tables and walls, facing a fixed 180 (moonwalking) and playing "Sit" while moving.
   Fix: on Seating, the group follows Mira's actor in a short trailing file. Once seated, any guest who is not at
   their seat walks a tile path (Layout.Path, cached per group) to the table, facing the direction of travel with the
   Walk clip, and sits only on arrival.
2. Guests pop out. At Gone (paid, or gave up in the queue or at the table) they vanish on that frame. Fix:
   presentation-only leavers walk to the door and despawn there (fade or step off-screen).
3. Queue overlap. The queue spot uses g.Id%4 and only i%2, so groups stack on each other and four or six guests share
   two spots. Fix: slots by queue order (Queue phase, sorted by Arrival), one row per group, guests side by side. The
   line shuffles forward smoothly when a group leaves it.
4. Plates appear late. A dish shows only once the WHOLE table is Eating. Show each guest's plate when their ticket is
   Delivered, and remove it at Payment.
5. Carry prop. The fish plate shows for the whole Serve/Clear task, including the empty walk to the pass, and drinks
   carry a fish plate. Show it only on the carrying leg, with the matching model (drink, coffee, dirty plate).
6. Staff motion. 4-connected tiles give robotic 90-degree turns, and two staff on one tile overlap exactly. Round the
   corners in presentation (chase a look-ahead point, or similar) and push apart staff who share a spot.
7. REPORT ONLY, no tuning: in RestaurantChecks, a balance table for 20 seeds x crew {Mira+captain, +1 cook,
   +cook+waiter, +cook+waiter+barista}. Columns: served/guests, gave-up, left at door, cold dishes, mean stars,
   net coins, and each staff member's idle %. Save it to docs/screens/restaurant-feel-1/balance.md. Claude does the
   tuning from it.
