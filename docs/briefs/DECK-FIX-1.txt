DECK-FIX-1 — Codex 1 (Windows) — three fixes to the tier-0/1 sort flow from Mo's play test (2026-10-24)

GOAL (Mo): "keep should be white not throw, the captain automatically collects his fish to sort unless
turned off in settings, also when you sort and click go home the keep throw keeps on screen, that needs fixing".

1. Colours: Keep = white secondary button, Throw = orange primary button. Same positions (Throw left, Keep right).
   Code: VoyageDeck.cs InterfaceSortDecision() (swap coastalUi.Primary/Secondary only).
2. Auto-carry: when the captain is idle on a deck-graph hull and a rod basket holds an unclaimed fish, the
   captain carries it to the sort table automatically (same path as the Carry bubble: CaptainHaulFrom(basket),
   Manning = null, AwaitingCast = false). Do not interrupt the captain while manning a rod with a fish on the line
   (Bite/Reeling) or while in the strike minigame. New setting "Auto-carry catch" On/Off in Settings
   (VoyageMenus.cs InterfaceSettings, PlayerPrefs "catchline.autoCarry", default On, same Row() style as Tips).
   With it Off the existing Carry bubble flow stays as is.
3. Lingering buttons: after sorting and pressing Head home, Keep/Throw stay on screen. They must disappear as
   soon as there is no fish waiting on a sort table and on any mode change (heading home, travel, harbour).
   Find why: InterfaceSortDecision is also called in the travel branch of VoyageInterface.RefreshInterface, and
   FlushDeckGraph/Scheduler may still report a fish in sort.In while sailing. Decide on travel behaviour: while
   heading home, no Keep/Throw at all (FlushDeckGraph already stores keepers) — remove the call there if that
   is the cause, and make sure no stale UI element survives (CoastalCanvas Begin/End must drop unused ids).
