PACK-DECOR-2 — glowing warm light fixtures for the restaurant (Codex 2, WSL CLI)

WHY
Claude just rebuilt the restaurant's look: an evening grade with real shadows, a deep
dusk sea and HDR bloom (commit 5352e7e; see docs/screens/restaurant-mood/r155/
day-service-late-1440.png). The room now glows — but the light FIXTURES themselves do
not emit. Lanterns, sconces and string lights are lit like ordinary props, so they read
as cold metal in a warm room. This brief gives the restaurant a set of fixed-placement
light fixtures whose lamp element is authored to GLOW, so Claude can make it emissive and
the new bloom makes them read as real warm lamplight.

The accepted TableCandle (RESTAURANT-UPGRADES-1) is your reference for a glowing element:
a small separate flame/bulb mesh on its own bright material. Do the same here.

BUILD these stems (one Unity FBX each, drop-in like PACK-DECOR-1):
  PendantLamp      hanging ceiling lamp, warm shade, glowing bulb under it        (ceiling/floor-anchored post ok)
  WallSconce       wall lamp, metal arm + glowing globe                            (wall, front -Z)
  HangingLantern   harbour lantern, glazed panes with a glowing core              (wall or hook)
  StringLightsLong a longer warm bulb strand (8-10 bulbs), each bulb glowing       (wall, like your StringLights but bulbs glow)
  TableLamp        small brass table lamp, cream shade, glowing bulb              (floor/table footprint)
  Brazier          terrace coal/heat brazier, glowing embers                      (floor)

CRITICAL — the glow element:
  - The part that emits (bulb, globe, flame, embers, lit lantern panes) MUST be a SEPARATE
    mesh with its OWN material, named with the suffix _Glow (e.g. PendantLamp_Glow). Give
    that material a bright ivory-to-amber base colour (roughly #FFE6B0..#FFC266). Everything
    else uses the normal warm-wood / navy / brass / cream pack atlas, same as PACK-DECOR-1.
  - Keep the glow mesh simple and readable at the game camera (a sphere/panel/flame), facing
    the room. Claude binds emission to the _Glow material so it blooms; you do NOT need to set
    emission yourself, just isolate it as its own mesh+material and colour it bright.
  - No baked lightmaps, no real lights, no render-floor / wall-swatch / camera geometry in the FBX.

CONVENTIONS (identical to PACK-DECOR-1): Unity Y-up, 1 unit = 1 m. Floor pieces pivot at the
floor-footprint centre; wall pieces pivot at the wall contact plane, front facing -Z, hung
~1.4-1.8 m; footprint 1x1 m unless the piece is obviously smaller. Root transform identity,
axis conversion baked, atlas png beside the FBX, warm-wood/navy/teal/cream + brass palette,
no diner-orange. Exact same exporter call as your PACK-DECOR-1 FBXs.
