## Catch and economy

Net generation ends at remaining kilograms; there is no5fish limit or hidden64fish
cap. Every specimen stays inside its local species' weight range. Deep nets favour
large specimens with greater per-kg prices, then fill residual space with local
school fish. Normal nets take many small school fish. Both obey the same hard mass
ceiling. Richness can reduce realized yield; equipment capacity stays10/15kg.
Individual hold storage retains the approved single-whole-fish final overflow rule;
net generation itself cannot exceed its gear limit. No fake weight clipping of fish.

3–4large fish is typical where local sizes support it, not a forced fish count.
The harbour has small species; the deep channel has much heavier cod/eels. Actual
200seed distribution measurements and100kg illustrative sales are in screens/net023.

Only tier3 now depletes grounds by weight (.0035richness/kg rather than per fish)
and uses5kg as the market-pressure reference rather than.5kg. Other boats retain
starter balance. Sale preview copies hull as well as market/upgrades, so actual
payout follows the same calculation. Freshness and contract bonuses are unchanged.
