# HUD-008 — cohesive coastal interface

Owner: Codex 1 (Windows app), 16 October 2026.

Mo asked for a complete stylized fishing HUD and proper shop screen, replacing
scattered panels. He selected illustrated categories, roomy items and a smaller
harbour view. The supplied goal renders, Fredoka and coastal kit remain the style
authority. No new bitmap was needed: the complete kit already supplies panels,
buttons, illustrated shop signs, icons, bobbers/chart and crew/fish portraits.

## Delivered interface

- One connected fishing instrument: place, hold quantity/value, capacity, food,
  bait. Contract progress beneath; crew in a single navy rail. The boat stays
  central. World-attached Fish/Watch/Carry controls and a unified bite/cast/reel
  instrument retain the existing actions. Non-blocking catch portrait/value.
- Full harbour shop with illustrated category navigation and headers, roomy
  two-column product displays, explicit prices/owned/prerequisite/shortfall
  states, scrolling, and a smaller harbour/departure area.
- Market sale and fish log/prices; chart selection/purchase and contracts;
  crew hiring/current contracts; gear/refit; bait single/bulk packs, food, ice/
  provisions upgrades, fuel and squid preference. Uses existing game-state
  methods and fees. Existing confirmed return/pause/reset protection preserved.
- Retained native Unity Canvas components share sliced artwork, typography,
  colours, hit states and safe-area scaling. Gameplay/shop IMGUI drawing is
  retired; menus/confirmation helpers remain. Phone targets enlarged and larger
  type supported. World zoom ignores scrolling over the interface.
- Corrected authored fish display scale against the existing species length;
  native catches no longer cover the camera with centimetre-scale FBX geometry.
  No fish assets, stats or rewards changed.
