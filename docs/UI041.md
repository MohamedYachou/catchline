# UI041 — Gemini UI pack and fish integration

Mo asked to preserve the liked pack direction, polish every picture, fix escaping letters and awful fish eyes, integrate the UI, and make its missing fish real game content.

## Assets and interface

The original Downloads ZIP is preserved. All39PNGs were inspected:9HUD pieces,2icons,6blank card templates,6glows,6populated previews,8fish and2mockups. Clean text-free components retain their approved cream/navy artwork. All8portraits were edited with imagegen: natural small dark eyes/highlights, rounded sculpted shading, cleaner contours, complete fish silhouettes and transparent backgrounds. Source images remain in the original extracted pack; generated-file receipts are under gemini/ui041.

The working game uses those sprites through its existing ArtLibrary/CoastalCanvas, with no disconnected ScriptableObject species system. Fishing has live day/elapsed time/wallet, selected ground, kg hold/progress, supplies/freshness/estimated haul, real crew activities and a Boat/Crew/Map/Gear dock. Boat opens the prep station on the Prep fisher; Crew opens its real duties; Map/Gear show current location/equipment at sea and explain port-only changes. They do not teleport the boat or buy upgrades at sea. Existing harbour shops, departure, controls, minigames and modals inherit the pack's shared cream panels/navy buttons/progress pieces. Existing illustrated map, water and boat assets remain.

Catch cards use the six supplied blank templates and live names, weights, estimated values, size/rarity and achievement labels. Portraits fit inside the left window. Text has fitted type and a true parent RectMask2D. UGUI ignores masks on the same object as their Graphic, so text lives inside a stretched child. Card layout leaves the captain's return/duties/dock actions accessible. Crew catches remain quiet; nets retain one aggregate receipt and last-haul review. Coins are awarded only by actual sale.

Free scraps remain small herring/mackerel; bait cannot import another ground's species. Nets still enforce their hard mass limit, so large fish require rods rather than silently overfilling a net.

Their distinct runtime meshes include rounded bodies, dorsal/pectoral/tail fins, flush dark eyes, ray wings/whip tail/spots, puffer spots/spines and leviathan eye patches. Generated per-fish fin meshes are released when that fish is destroyed; shared body meshes are cached by species. Mackerel/cod keep their existing world models and get refined portraits.
